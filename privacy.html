Your words stay close.

Your scripts stay in your local library. Your speech is processed on your device. Here is how Impromptu handles your information.

Your scripts

Impromptu stores scripts, your reading position, and appearance preferences in the app's local storage. It does not send those scripts to a developer-operated server and does not provide accounts or automatic cloud sync.

Importing a file creates a local copy without changing the original. Exporting creates a copy that you can share using the destination you choose. That destination's privacy practices apply to the exported copy. Your device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Your microphone

When you tap Start for a ready AI Follow session, the app requests microphone permission so the script can follow your speech. Speech recognition runs on your device. The app does not save microphone audio as a recording and does not upload audio or transcripts to a transcription service. A listening indicator appears while microphone input is active. Opening the app and preparing speech assets do not activate the microphone. Audio capture starts only after you explicitly start a ready session.

When the app opens, it prepares Apple's on-device speech system, which may need to download language assets before AI Follow is available. Apple manages those assets. Start does not initiate a model download. Downloading a language model requires a network connection; using installed, available speech assets does not send your voice to a recognition server.

Backups and deletion

To protect your work, Impromptu retains a previous save and preserves damaged files when recovering a script. Deleting a script from the library removes its current save, previous save, and recovery copies associated with that script. Unreadable orphaned recovery files and damaged preference copies stay in the app's local storage until the app is deleted. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies you exported and device backups are controlled separately through your chosen apps and system settings.

Analytics, advertising, and purchases

Impromptu has no advertising, purchases, subscriptions, developer analytics, tracking SDKs, or account system. Apple may provide operating-system or App Store services under its own privacy policies and your device settings.

Support and this website

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending confidential scripts or recordings unless you intend to share them. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. The site has no added analytics, advertising, contact forms, or tracking scripts. The app does not send script text or microphone audio to this website. Read the GitHub privacy statement for details about the hosting service.
